Privacy

your kitchen stays in your kitchen

Fridge Door Companion has no account, no server of its own, no analytics, and no cookies. This page explains exactly what it stores and where.

What the app stores

Your categories, the amounts in your fridge, and your shopping list are saved in your browser's own storage (today the fdc-state-v1 key in localStorage; the planned move to IndexedDB changes where, not who can read it). That data never leaves your device unless you export it.

The app also caches its own files in a service worker cache so it can open with no network. Those cached files are the app itself — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, icons — not your kitchen.

What we never do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or email collection.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising.
  • No cookies and no fingerprinting.
  • No third-party fonts, scripts, or CDNs. Every file is served from this site.
  • No server-side copy of your kitchen. We cannot read it, and we cannot recover it for you.

Export and import

Export kitchen writes a JSON file to your device with your categories, stock, and shopping list. Where that file goes is up to you — treat it as you would any personal note, because it describes your home. Import kitchen reads a file you choose, validates it, and replaces the kitchen in this browser only after you confirm.

Deleting your data

Reset kitchen under Your data clears this app's stored key and starts over. Clearing your browser's site data does the same, and also removes the offline cache. There is no remote copy to delete because there is no remote copy.

Hosting

The app is served as a static site from GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees the usual request metadata (IP address, user agent, requested file) when your browser loads the page. That is the host's standard logging, not something the app adds or reads. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Recipes and attribution

The built-in recipes and ingredient data live in data/ in the source repository. Provenance is recorded as the collection grows; third-party material will be attributed when it is added (roadmap M2).

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
